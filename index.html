Mission: Conduct a Valid Meeting

Complete the seven stations. The activities follow the presentation's core framework and turn the content into sorting, calculation, decision-making, sequencing and case-based tasks.

Station 3 · Sequencing Challenge

Run the Meeting

Arrange the cards into a sensible classroom sequence from preparation through record-keeping. The order is a teaching sequence derived from the presentation, not a claim that every step has a single statutory ordering.
